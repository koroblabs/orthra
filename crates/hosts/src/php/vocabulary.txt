# Words of PHP and its ecosystem that are words in a PHP file, whatever a dictionary says. A
# dictionary word needs no line here. One word per line, sorted, so two additions merge without a
# conflict.
#
# Most are the parts of PHP's own functions, which glue words together: `mb_strtolower` gives
# `strtolower`. They were taken from the global functions that mago-prelude 1.58.2 (MIT OR
# Apache-2.0) declares for the everyday extensions: core, standard, date, pcre, spl, json, ctype,
# mbstring, filter, hash, random, curl, openssl, iconv, zlib, fileinfo, session, reflection,
# tokenizer, phar and opcache. The rest were added by hand: extension and tool names, and the words
# PHP coined (`autoloading`, `heredoc`, `isset`).
